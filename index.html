JRDev Uploader

JRDev Uploader is a personal desktop tool that runs on the creator's own computer. It prepares short vertical videos (clips and "What if...?" curiosity videos) and sends the finished video to the creator's own TikTok account, where the creator reviews, edits and publishes it inside the TikTok app.

  • Sign in with TikTok (Login Kit) to connect the creator's own account.
  • Upload a finished video to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft (Content Posting API, video.upload).
  • Everything runs locally. There is no public sign-up and no server that stores user data.

Em português: o JRDev Uploader é uma ferramenta pessoal que roda no computador do criador. Ela prepara vídeos curtos e envia o vídeo pronto como rascunho para a conta do próprio criador no TikTok, onde ele revisa e publica.
