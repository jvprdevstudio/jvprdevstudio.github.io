Privacy Policy

JRDev Uploader · Effective date: November 13, 2026

1. Summary

JRDev Uploader runs entirely on your own computer. JRDev does not operate a server that receives, stores or sells your data. The only external service the app talks to for TikTok is TikTok itself.

2. What the app accesses

  • Basic TikTok profile (user.info.basic): your display name, avatar and account identifier, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Video upload (video.upload): the app sends the video file you choose to your TikTok inbox as a draft. It does not read your existing videos, messages, followers or analytics.
  • Access tokens: the tokens TikTok issues when you connect are saved only in a local file on your computer, so you don't have to sign in every time.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't collect your data on any JRDev server.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data with third parties.
  • We don't use your data for advertising or tracking.

4. Your control

You can disconnect at any time: use the disconnect option in the app (which deletes the local tokens) or remove JRDev Uploader from the authorized apps in your TikTok settings. Deleting the app's data folder removes everything it saved.

5. TikTok

Videos you upload are handled by TikTok under the TikTok Privacy Policy.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children and should only be used by account owners who meet TikTok's age requirements.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the effective date above shows the latest version. Questions: jvprdev.studio@gmail.com
